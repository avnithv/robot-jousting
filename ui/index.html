Keyframes

Pose each arm by hand and capture keyframes into a timeline: Unfreeze an arm (torque off), move it, Capture; Freeze holds it where it is. Two keys (start, end) are usually enough. Save builds KF_<name> (arm A) and KF_<name>@B (arm B) into the move list, so they play on either arm, on both together, or in a TURN.

Collision calibration

Pick the attacker arm, its attack, and the other arm's move. Start parks the defender at its end pose, brings the attacker to its strike start (normal speed), then creeps through the strike at the calibration speed. Press STOP the moment the blades meet; the stop point (backed off 3%) is saved for that pair and the attacker holds there. Retreat brings both arms back to rest slowly.
